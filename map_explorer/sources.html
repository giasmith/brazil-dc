Data & Sources

Every dataset behind the Sovereign Compute Nexus model, with provenance, license, and citation

← Back to the explorer

The model joins thirteen datasets onto a single H3 decision grid. Ten come from outside — government agencies, satellites, and open databases — and three are built inside the workspace from the others. This page records where each one came from, how it was acquired, what it contributes, and the terms it is used under. Everything here is reproducible from the scripts in the brazil-dc repository.

Territory & law Social impact

The legal map. Protected areas, indigenous territories, and state boundaries are treated as hard exclusions: a cell that overlaps one is out before any objective is scored.

Brazilian territorial layers

IBGE · MMA · FUNAI, packaged by IPEA's geobr project Public data via geobr (MIT)
What it is
Four simplified boundary layers: 27 states, 5,573 municipalities (2025), 3,001 conservation units (March 2025), and 637 indigenous lands (2025). EPSG:4326.
Acquired via
Parquet files from the geobr prepared-data releases (github.com/ipea/geobr_prep_data, v2.0.0), merged into one GeoPackage in a section of preprocessing.ipynb.
Output
clean_data/brazil_territorial_layers.gpkg · brazil_territorial_layers_metadata.json
Used for
Phase 1 hard exclusions (protected overlap, indigenous overlap, outside-state cells); Phase 3 boundary-review buffer (2.5 km); state polygons and overlays on both maps.
Citation
Pereira, R. H. M. & Gonçalves, C. N. (2019). geobr: Loads shapefiles of official spatial data sets of Brazil. IPEA. Underlying boundaries © IBGE; conservation units © MMA/CNUC; indigenous lands © FUNAI.

Energy & grid Energy

Where the power is, where it is being wasted, and what the wires can carry.

Official ONS grid topology and generation

Operador Nacional do Sistema Elétrico (ONS), Dados Abertos Open data portal · verify terms
What it is
Brazil's real transmission network as published by the grid operator: substations, lines, installed capacity, generation, load and energy balance. 1,705 buses, 1,838 active branches, 4,626 generating units, 198,138 MW installed.
Acquired via
Direct pulls from the ONS open-data bucket (ons-aws-prod-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com). Thirteen dataset folders under data/ons/: subestacao, linha_transmissao, capacidade-geracao, geracao_usina_2_ho, carga_energia_di, balanco_energia_subsistema_ho, usina_conjunto, usina_pqu, modalidade_usina, and the four restricao_coff_* folders below. Built by scripts/build_ons_official_grid.py.
Output
clean_data/ons_official_grid/ — GeoPackage model, bus and branch CSVs, generation assignments, ONS-vs-GEM energy mix.
Used for
The authoritative topology. Every distance-to-substation and distance-to-line constraint in Phase 4 is measured against it. DC power-flow approximation, not AC optimal power flow.
Citation
ONS — Operador Nacional do Sistema Elétrico. Dados Abertos. dados.ons.org.br. Retrieved May 2026.

ONS renewable curtailment records

ONS, Dados Abertos — restrição de operação por constrained-off Open data portal · verify terms
What it is
Two resolutions. Aggregate: monthly constrained-off files for wind and solar, 82 files covering October 2021 – May 2026; 502.3 TWh generated, 63.5 TWh curtailed (11.2% of potential). Detail: half-hourly plant and conjunto files with estimated vs. verified generation and weather fields; 91,985,149 rows across 1,593 plant IDs.
Acquired via
restricao_coff_eolica_tm, restricao_coff_fotovoltaica_tm and their _detail_tm counterparts. Processed by scripts/analyze_ons_curtailment_history.py and analyze_ons_curtailment_detail.py.
Output
clean_data/ons_curtailment_history/ · clean_data/ons_curtailment_detail/
Used for
Evidence that transmission scarcity is real and priced; state-level curtailment totals feed the curtailment-opportunity objective in Phase 4 and the Brazil overview map.
Citation
ONS. Restrição de Operação por Constrained-off de Usinas Eólicas e Fotovoltaicas. Dados Abertos. Retrieved May 2026.

Energy asset inventory (GEM-style tracker)

Global Energy Monitor–style Portal Energético Tracker workbook CC BY 4.0 (GEM trackers)
What it is
Project-level records for solar, wind, hydropower, bioenergy, oil & gas, coal, nuclear and geothermal plants, plus pipelines, terminals and extraction fields. Workbook dated 2026-03-24: 34 sheets, 19 with data, 22,323 rows, 49 empty columns dropped, no rows lost. Coverage is Latin America–wide; every script filters to Brazil on the country column.
Acquired via
Workbook held in OneDrive; cleaned into 19 CSVs by the original notebook pipeline.
Output
clean_data/energy/*.csv · cleaning_report.json
Used for
Nearby-renewable capacity per cell; the proposed-generation stress scenario; the "Power plants" layer on both maps (2,523 operating, 172 under construction, 3,376 planned at 5 MW and up). Also the input to the GEM-style reference grid below.
Citation
Global Energy Monitor. Global Solar, Wind, Hydropower, Bioenergy, Oil & Gas, Coal, Nuclear and Geothermal Power Trackers. Data released under CC BY 4.0; cite the tracker and release date used.

Where compute already sits. The model uses these points as fiber and interconnection anchors; the curated list adds status, capacity and cooling detail.

OpenStreetMap contributors, via the Overpass API ODbL 1.0
What it is
Elements in Brazil tagged telecom=data_center or telecom=data_centre: 57 strictly tagged features.
Acquired via
scripts/download_osm_brazil_data_centers.py against overpass-api.de and mirrors.
Output
clean_data/osm_data_centers/

The tag-pure, conservative layer; merged with PeeringDB into the 336-point anchor set.
Citation

PeeringDB facilities

PeeringDB public facility API Public API · verify data terms
What it is
302 Brazilian interconnection facilities with coordinates, network counts and IX counts; merged with the OSM-only points above into 336 unique locations.
Acquired via
scripts/download_brazil_idc_points.py — peeringdb.com/api/fac?country=BR plus the public KMZ coordinate file.
Output
clean_data/idc_data_centers/
Used for
The "fiber anchor" the model measures distance to: within 50 km is a hard constraint in Phase 4, and proximity is a minimized objective. Records are user-maintained; validate before site-specific claims.
Citation
PeeringDB. Facility records for Brazil. peeringdb.com. Retrieved May 2026.

Curated Brazilian data-center list

Compiled for this project from operator pages and industry press Project compilation · sources per row
What it is
110 facilities with operator, location, status, capacity (MW), cooling and metrics notes, and a documentation link per row. 93 commissioned, 2 under construction, 14 planned; capacity known for 96, "undisclosed" for 13, "100+" for one.
File
map_explorer/data/brazilian_data_centers.csv
Placement
60 rows carry exact coordinates. 43 name only a city and sit at the centroid of that city's located rows; 6 name only a state and sit at the state capital. Each record shows its precision. One row (V.tal Barranquilla, Colombia) is outside Brazil and is not mapped.
Used for
The "Data centers — curated" layer: status flags, capacity-sized markers, and the facility record panel. Not used by the siting model itself.
Citation
Each row cites its own source URL in the Official Documentation column. Treat capacities and cooling notes as operator-reported.

Land & water Land

What is on the ground, and where the water is.

Land cover and surface water rasters

MapBiomas Brazil (class-code family), 30 m, 2024 CC BY 4.0 · citation required
What it is
A 30-meter land-cover raster for 2024 (forest, savanna, pasture, urban and 26 other classes — 30-class legend) and a 2024 surface-water raster. BigTIFF, EPSG:4326.
Acquired via
Downloaded from MapBiomas and held in OneDrive; mounted in place as GDAL VRT mosaics by scripts/load_lulc_water_layers.py. The land-cover source file is still the browser partial-download (Unconfirmed 614937.crdownload) — readable, but replace before publication.
Output
clean_data/lulc_water/ — VRTs, class legend, per-state area summaries.
Used for
Land-cover vulnerability in the Phase 2 proxy; surface water as a hard exclusion; dominant class per cell (centroid sample). State summaries use a downsampled grid.
Citation
MapBiomas Project. Collection of Annual Land Use and Land Cover Maps of Brazil, 2024. mapbiomas.org. Licensed CC BY 4.0; cite the collection number of the file used.

Satellite Remote sensing

The real measurement behind the degradation risk score.

Sentinel-2 optical and Sentinel-1 radar composites

ESA Copernicus, accessed through Google Earth Engine Copernicus free, full and open
What it is
Sentinel-2 (COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED): bands B2, B3, B4, B8, B11, B12 plus NDVI, NDWI and NBR, cast to Float32. Sentinel-1 (COPERNICUS/S1_GRD): VV, VH, VV−VH and VV/VH. Both at 10 m, quarterly composites, 2021-10-01 to 2026-05-01, clipped to the 50 km × 50 km Ceará box.
Acquired via
Export tasks submitted under Earth Engine project edwc-483823 by scripts/request_sentinel_gee_exports.py (Python) and gee/request_sentinel_ceara_exports.js (Code Editor); 38 tasks to Google Drive. Zonal means per H3 cell computed in Colab against the Drive-mounted GeoTIFFs. The feature table is phase2_rs_features_for_solver.csv.
Output
Per-cell NDVI, NDWI, VV, VH for all 2,808 cells → clean_data/sovereign_compute_nexus/phase2_rs/
Used for
Phase 2 degradation probability Pdeg: low NDVI as vegetation stress, high NDWI as wetness sensitivity, VV/VH as radar pressure. Replaces the earlier PCA reconstruction-error proxy, which is retained as a fallback.
Citation
Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021–2026), processed by ESA. Accessed via Google Earth Engine — Gorelick, N., et al. (2017). Google Earth Engine: Planetary-scale geospatial analysis for everyone. Remote Sensing of Environment, 202, 18–27.

Policy corpus Policy

The rules the model has to obey, turned into bounded categories it cannot invent values for.

ReData and land-protection policy corpus

Brazilian federal government sources and peer-reviewed literature, curated Public documents · articles by DOI
What it is
36 documents: 12 government sources on ReData, data-center incentives and digital infrastructure (Planalto, Senado, Câmara, Receita Federal, Fazenda, MDIC, MME, MMA, Anatel); 15 on indigenous rights, consultation duties, protected areas and environmental licensing (Constitution arts. 225 and 231, Lei 6.001/1973, Decreto 1.775/1996, Lei 14.701/2023, Decreto 10.088/2019 on ILO 169, Lei 9.985/2000 SNUC, CONAMA 428/2010, Lei 6.938/1981, LC 140/2011, FUNAI guidance); and 9 peer-reviewed articles on indigenous lands and protected areas as conservation regimes.
File
docs/phase3_redata_policy_corpus.md and .json — each entry carries issuer, date, URL or DOI, and its role in Phase 3.
Used for
Phase 3 bounded classifier: which legal conditions trigger a hard exclusion, a 2.5 km human-review buffer, or a stringency weight of 1, 5 or 100. Deterministic rules, not a language model.
Citation
Cite individual documents from the corpus file. Journal entries were identified from publisher and DOI records and should be re-verified against full text before publication.

Known issues Read before citing a number

Found while re-deriving every figure from the source files. Each is documented in the advisor memo; none has been silently corrected.

Zero-distance bug in Phase 4. float(row.get(field, np.inf) or np.inf) reads a distance of exactly 0 km as infinite, so 147 cells sitting directly on a transmission line were excluded for being too far from one. Correcting it raises the feasible set from 819 to 966 and promotes a cell scoring 74.33, above the current top recommendation of 73.52. The explorer defaults to the published run and offers a "Fix the 0 km bug" toggle.
One Phase 4 objective does no work. objective_curtailment_shortfall is identically 0 across all 819 feasible cells, because curtailment enters only at state resolution and the whole box is in Ceará. The frontier is effectively a five-objective sort.
Degradation probability is clipped at 0.90. 355 cells (12.6%) sit exactly at the ceiling; ranking information in the high-risk tail is lost.
Water share is binary. water_surface_share_proxy takes only 0 and 1 — a centroid hit test, not an areal share — and drives a hard exclusion on 64 cells.
Sentinel ingest log points at the wrong Drive folder. The log reports 0 of 38 exports found while the export symlink and the solver features show otherwise; the repository cannot presently state which composites produced the features.
Land-cover raster is a partial download. The file still carries a .crdownload suffix. It is readable, but replace it with the completed MapBiomas file before publication-grade zonal statistics.
Curated data-center list: 50 of 110 rows have no coordinates. The gaps fall disproportionately on the hyperscalers and government facilities. Those rows are placed at city or state centroids and flagged as approximate on the map.
Resolved. The energy-inventory tables are Latin America–wide; the pipeline scripts already filter to Brazil, and the map layers now do too. Two README figures (22,874 → 22,323 rows; 15/12 → 12/15 corpus split) were transposed and are corrected in the repository README.
