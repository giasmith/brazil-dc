# Phase 3 ReData and Land-Protection Policy Corpus

Last updated: 2026-09-23 (schema `2026-09-status-aware`)

This is the curated source list used to expand Phase 3 from a notes-driven bounded classifier into a ReData-aware policy corpus. It separates data-center incentive law from hard land, water, indigenous, traditional-community, and protected-area constraints. Every document now carries a lifecycle `status`; the automated sweep in `scripts/build_policy_corpus.py` uses this file as its gold set and `python scripts/build_policy_corpus.py validate-corpus` enforces the schema. Journal sources were identified from publisher/DOI records (the scite MCP was unavailable when they were added) and should be verified again before final publication.

## What Changed On 2026-09-23

- **Redata is law.** PL 278/2026 was sanctioned as **Lei 15.504, de 15 de setembro de 2026** (DOU 2026-09-16). MP 1.318/2025 is recorded as `lapsed`; PL 278/2026 as `enacted`; the Lei as the only `redata_core_law` document `in_force`.
- **Lei 15.190/2025 (Lei Geral do Licenciamento Ambiental)** was missing from the licensing category. Added with status `veto_overridden` (52 veto items overridden on 2025-11-27; in force since Feb 2026 with STF challenges).
- **Energy-access layer** added: Decreto 12.772/2025 (PNAST access auctions) and ANEEL REN 1.122/2025 (large-load guarantees).
- **Telecom/environment signals** added: Anatel Res. 780/2025 (+ Acórdão 58/2026 suspending art. 6) and CONAMA Moção 147/2026.
- **State fiscal layer** added: Convênio ICMS 38/2026 (CONFAZ) with status `unverified` — its text was not read; per-state adhesion is the field that matters.
- **Three 2026 Câmara bills** on data-center licensing and water (PL 5209, 5247, 5319/2026) added as `proposed`.
- Rows whose `status_note` says a URL or date was not opened must be checked before publication.

## How Phase 3 Uses This Corpus

- **ReData documents** define the data-center incentive problem: fiscal benefits, clean/renewable energy, sustainability criteria, water/energy efficiency, domestic capacity, and digital sovereignty. Only `in_force` instruments feed the opportunity layer.
- **Energy-access and regulatory documents** (PNAST, ANEEL, Anatel) turn grid proximity into an access-scarcity constraint and add conformity duties.
- **Indigenous and traditional-community documents** define hard exclusions, human-review buffers, consultation duties, and Marco Temporal precaution.
- **Protected-area and environmental documents** define conservation-unit exclusions, licensing review (now under Lei 15.190/2025), protected-space logic, and environmental liability.
- **State fiscal documents** decide where the federal incentive is actually amplified (ICMS) — an adoption map, not a national constant.
- **Peer-reviewed literature** supports the policy choice to treat indigenous lands and protected lands as substantive socio-ecological constraints rather than tunable penalties.

## Lifecycle Chain (Relations)

Relations are stored per document (`relations: [{type, target, evidence}]`) and exported by the corpus builder as `relations.jsonl`. The Redata chain reads:

```text
MPV 1.318/2025 (lapsed) <-replaces- PL 278/2026 (enacted) -enacted_as-> Lei 15.504/2026 (in_force) -amends-> Lei 11.196/2005, Lei 15.211/2025
                                                                Lei 15.504/2026 <-cites- Convênio ICMS 38/2026 (unverified; adoption per state)
Res. Anatel 780/2025 (in_force) -suspended_by-> Acórdão 58/2026 (art. 6 only)
Lei 15.190/2025 (veto_overridden) <-cites- PL 5209/2026 (proposed)
```

## Methodological Translation Into Phase 3

The bounded classifier keeps these documents out of the objective function as tunable prose. Instead, it translates them into strict categories:

- `Critical`: direct overlap with indigenous land, protected land, water class, outside state boundary, or high remote-sensing degradation probability.
- `Medium`: protected/indigenous fray cells, near-boundary review buffers, infrastructure pressure near sensitive lands, elevated `p_deg_rs`, or unknown grid-access status under PNAST/REN 1.122.
- `Low`: cells without current policy triggers.

This preserves the key governance claim: ReData incentives can be modeled as an opportunity layer, but constitutional, indigenous, traditional-community, protected-area, water, and licensing constraints must remain prior feasibility constraints.

Status awareness matters for the claim: `run_scn_phase3_policy.py` now reports `has_redata_core_law_in_force` (true only for Lei 15.504/2026) alongside the older presence flag, so a corpus whose only Redata instrument is a lapsed MP cannot pass silently. Note also Art. 11-J of Lei 15.504/2026: the regime has a five-year term but the PIS/Pasep, Cofins and IPI benefits produce effects only until 2026-12-31 (tax-reform transition) — the incentive layer's duration should be modeled from the transposition into CBS/IBS, not from the five-year clause alone.

## Maintaining This Corpus

```bash
python scripts/build_policy_corpus.py validate-corpus            # schema + lifecycle consistency
python scripts/build_policy_corpus.py sweep --since 2024-01-01    # federal + regulatory + state sweep
python scripts/build_policy_corpus.py recall                      # recall of the sweep against this gold set
```

New candidates surface in `clean_data/sovereign_compute_nexus/phase3_policy_corpus/candidates_for_curation.jsonl`; promote them here by hand with a `status`, a `status_note` naming the evidence, and `relations`. See `docs/policy_corpus_pipeline.md`.
