# Phase 3 ReData and Land-Protection Policy Corpus

Last updated: 2026-05-28

This is the working source list used to expand Phase 3 from a notes-driven bounded classifier into a ReData-aware policy corpus. The corpus separates data-center incentive law from hard land, water, indigenous, traditional-community, and protected-area constraints. Scite full-text verification was not available in this session because the MCP quota was exhausted, so journal sources are listed from publisher/DOI records and should be verified again before final publication.

## How Phase 3 Uses This Corpus

- **ReData documents** define the data-center incentive problem: fiscal benefits, clean/renewable energy, sustainability criteria, water/energy efficiency, domestic capacity, and digital sovereignty.
- **Indigenous and traditional-community documents** define hard exclusions, human-review buffers, consultation duties, and Marco Temporal precaution.
- **Protected-area and environmental documents** define conservation-unit exclusions, licensing review, protected-space logic, and environmental liability.
- **Peer-reviewed literature** supports the policy choice to treat indigenous lands and protected lands as substantive socio-ecological constraints rather than tunable penalties.

## Methodological Translation Into Phase 3

The bounded classifier should keep these documents out of the objective function as tunable prose. Instead, it translates them into strict categories:

- `Critical`: direct overlap with indigenous land, protected land, water class, outside state boundary, or high remote-sensing degradation probability.
- `Medium`: protected/indigenous fray cells, near-boundary review buffers, infrastructure pressure near sensitive lands, or elevated `p_deg_rs`.
- `Low`: cells without current policy triggers.

This preserves the key governance claim: ReData incentives can be modeled as an opportunity layer, but constitutional, indigenous, traditional-community, protected-area, water, and licensing constraints must remain prior feasibility constraints.
